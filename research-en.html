What we do

Research

We connect physical hazard simulation, earth observation, human behavior, and decision support to understand disaster impacts and improve how communities prepare and respond.

Research architecture

Connected research streams

Our research spans four connected themes: tsunami forecasting, agent-based modeling, Earth observation, and disaster Digital Twins. AI, machine learning, deep learning, reinforcement learning, large language models, scientific computing, and data fusion are used as appropriate to the research question.

Our distinctive contribution

From hazard observation to human response

A satellite image can show where damage occurred; a physical model can estimate where water may go; an agent-based model can test how people respond; and a Digital Twin can bring these layers together for planning and action.

Research principles

Useful beyond the paper

Research-grade

Models are tested, compared, and documented with explicit assumptions and limitations.

Practitioner-oriented

Outputs are designed for planning questions: timing, bottlenecks, exposure, and intervention.

Reusable

Open workflows, case studies, manuals, and software help others adapt the work.
